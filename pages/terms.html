Who we are

johnplusexams.com is run by John O'Brien ("we"). Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record for every order.

What you buy

A pack gives one person access to the papers in that pack for 12 months from the date of payment. A teacher licence also allows the buyer to use the papers with their own classes and to print copies for those students.

What you may not do

  • Share your account or your sign-in link with anyone else.
  • Copy, sell or publish the papers, PDFs or explanations, in whole or in part.
  • Upload the material to another website, shared drive or course platform.

The material

The papers are original practice material written for the C1 Advanced and B2 First exams. They are not official Cambridge papers. We are not affiliated with Cambridge University Press & Assessment.

Changes and availability

We may correct or improve papers during your access period. We aim to keep the site available at all times but cannot promise it will never be interrupted.
