Privacy

Last updated: [date]

What we store

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and so we know which pack you bought.
  • Your answers, scores and mistakes, so you can see your progress and try your mistakes again.
  • Which pack you bought and when your access ends.

Who handles it

  • Supabase stores accounts and progress (servers in the EU).
  • Lemon Squeezy handles payments. We never see your card details.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't use advertising trackers, and we only email you about your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@johnplusexams.com.
