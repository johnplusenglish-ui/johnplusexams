Refunds

Last updated: [date]

All sales are final. Your papers and PDFs are available to use and download as soon as you pay, so we can't take them back, and we don't give refunds once your pack is open.

Before you buy, try the free C1 paper: it is a complete paper and works exactly like the ones in the pack.

If something goes wrong

If the papers don't open, a PDF is missing, or you were charged twice, write to hello@johnplusexams.com with the email you paid with. We'll fix it, and if we can't, we'll refund you.

Your right to cancel

Under EU consumer law you normally have 14 days to cancel an online purchase. For digital content you can use straight away, that right ends when you agree at checkout to immediate access. This does not affect your rights if the content is faulty.
